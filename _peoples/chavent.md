**[Marie Chavent](https://marie-chavent.perso.math.cnrs.fr/)**  
is professor of statistics at Bordeaux University and member of the [Institut de Mathématiques de Bordeaux (IMB)](https://www.math.u-bordeaux.fr/fr/), 
and member of the Inria team [Astral](https://www.inria.fr/fr/astral). 
His research focuses on the development of statistical methods for dimension reduction,
clustering and multivariate data analysis.
