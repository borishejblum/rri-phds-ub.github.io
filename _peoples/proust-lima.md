**[Cécile Proust-Lima](https://www.bordeaux-population-health.center/author/cecile.proust-lima/)** coordinates the Public Health Data Science Network. She is a director of research in Biostatistics at [Inserm U1219 *Bordeaux Population Health*](https://www.bordeaux-population-health.center/), deputy director of the [Biostat team](https://www.bordeaux-population-health.center/the-teams/biostatistics/). She develops longitudinal statistical methodologies that leverage epidemiological cohort data to describe, explain and predict chronic disease progression. Her research interests cover joint modelling techniques, dynamic models, latent variable models, mediation analysis or machine learning techniques.
